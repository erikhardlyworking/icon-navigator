# Icon Navigator privacy policy

**Publisher:** Hardly Working  
**Contact:** workinghardlyforyou@gmail.com  
**Effective date:** 15 November 2026  
**Android package:** `dev.iconnavigator`

Icon Navigator helps you launch apps from configurable home-screen widgets. It works on your device without an account, advertising, analytics, or an app-operated server. The Android app does not request internet access and does not send your widget settings, installed-app information, or artwork to Hardly Working or other companies.

## Information used on your device

The app asks Android which apps in your current profile have launcher activities. It uses their package/activity identifiers, names and original icons to let you choose shortcuts and launch them. It does not read those apps' private contents, your contacts, messages, location, microphone, or camera.

Your selected apps, order, labels, sizes, widget layout and appearance preferences are saved in the app's private storage. If you choose a custom icon or background through Android's document picker, the app copies that selected image into its private storage to display it. It does not request unrestricted access to your photo library or storage.

Widget settings are provided to your Android launcher so it can display the widgets. Tapping an icon asks Android to open the activity you selected. Your launcher, Android system, and the apps you open have their own privacy practices. Icon Navigator does not receive the contents or account information of the apps it launches.

## Collection and sharing

The app does not transmit personal information off your device. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, tracking identifiers, crash-upload service, or account registration. Hardly Working does not collect or sell your installed-app list, widget configuration or selected images through Icon Navigator.

Automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup of the app's private data are disabled in the app's Android configuration. Device administrators, operating systems and services outside the app remain subject to their own controls and policies.

## Retention and deletion

Local settings and imported images stay on your device until you remove them where supported, clear Icon Navigator's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Removing a widget deletes that widget's configuration; imported files may remain in app storage for use by other widgets until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. There is no online account to delete and no remote copy of your widget data held by Hardly Working.

## Support and external links

If you email workinghardlyforyou@gmail.com, Hardly Working receives the address and information you choose to include and uses them to handle your request. Please avoid sending information you do not want included in that correspondence. Email is handled by your chosen email service and the publisher's email provider.

The public source and online policy are hosted on GitHub. Opening an external policy, source or support link uses your chosen browser or email app; those services have their own privacy policies. These links do not add analytics or network access to Icon Navigator itself.

## Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Questions about Icon Navigator or this policy can be sent to **workinghardlyforyou@gmail.com**.
